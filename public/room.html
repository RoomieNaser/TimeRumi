    • About Timerumi

    Hi! I'm Roomie from Roomix Cubing. I first created this project as a way for me to be able to livestream solving Rubik's cubes with my fans and having the best solves be shown on the leaderboard on an OBS overlay. Before this project, I had completely given up on cubing in its entirety and had lost interest. But something about livestreaming with my viewers made something click. It was fun again!

    The first iteration was.. subpar to say the least. A barebones structure with only manual time entry. So with very basic knowledge on HTML and CSS, and pretty much zero background in Javascript, I started to create what I now envisioned - a place where you can have competitions and hangout with your friends, all while at the comfort of your own home - learning as I go.

    One thing led to another, and I ended up adding a singleplayer mode for it to be used as a proper timer, a chat for people to hangout in, a statistics panel. Started designing things a lot more seriously, making sure the user gets the final say. Making everything feel intentional made the experience much nicer. For things I couldn't wrap my mind around - @the_swest helped contribute immensely.

    It all started to be fun again for me - cubing, development, design, streaming, making videos - everything. This project has given me a lot, but it has also taken a lot from me. I've built this alone in its entirety. There is a lot of time and energy that has gone into making my "Multiplayer Cubing" vision be brought into life. If you appreciate what this website does for you - consider donating from the landing page - I would sincerely appreciate any rewards for the sleepless nights I spent building this website.

    However, a better way to show support is spreading the word! I don't have enough reach to be able to bring this project to everyone who would appreciate it. Word of mouth goes a long way!
